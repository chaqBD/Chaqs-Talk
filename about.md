I'm Shakir, a doctoral researcher. This is my running research journal.

Two kinds of posts show up here:

- **Literature Review** — notable findings and arguments from papers I'm reading, condensed to their most cogent points.
- **Case Study** — real-world implementations of AI applied to specific supply chain and operations problems (demand forecasting, inventory optimization, logistics, procurement, risk/resilience, and so on), and what they reveal.

I update this regularly as my reading progresses. Feel free to subscribe via [RSS]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}).
